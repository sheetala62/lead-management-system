Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new lead?
Go to the Leads page and click Add Lead in the top right. Fill in the required fields (name, company, mobile, email) and click Save. The lead will appear in your pipeline immediately.
How do I change a lead's status?
Click Edit on any lead row to open the edit modal. Change the Lead Status field and save. You can also use Bulk Actions to change status on multiple leads at once.
How do I export leads to Excel?
On the Leads page, click Export → Export as CSV. The CSV file can be opened directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application. Active filters are applied to the export.
How do I import leads from a CSV?
Click Import on the Leads page. Download the template CSV to see the expected format, fill it in, then drag it into the import zone or browse to select the file. Click Import to process it.
Can I add multiple team members?
Yes. Go to Admin Panel and click Add Staff. You can create accounts with Staff, Manager, or Admin roles. Admins have full access; Staff can only view and manage leads.
How do I reset my password?
On the login page, click Forgot password? Enter your email or username and we will send a reset link. You can also change your password any time from My Profile → Change Password.
How do I configure email notifications?
Go to Settings → Email / SMTP and enter your SMTP credentials. You can test the connection with Send Test Email. Customise notification content in Settings → Email Templates.
What is the difference between Admin and Manager roles?
Admin has full access including settings, staff management, audit logs, and all leads. Manager can manage all leads and view reports but cannot access system settings. Staff can only work with leads assigned to them.
How do I set up WhatsApp notifications?
Go to Settings → WhatsApp. You need a WhatsApp Cloud API account. Enter your Phone Number ID, Bearer Token, and From Number, then enable it.
Can I use dark mode?
Yes! Click the moon icon in the top navigation bar to toggle between light and dark mode. Your preference is saved automatically.

About LeadMS CRM

LeadMS CRM is a professional lead management system built for growing sales teams. It provides a centralised workspace to capture leads, track every follow-up interaction, and analyse your pipeline in real time.

Built with Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, and a clean vanilla JS frontend, LeadMS is designed to be fast, reliable, and easy to self-host.
