Features

Everything your sales team needs

From lead capture to closing deals, LeadMS gives your team the tools to work faster and smarter.

Live Dashboard
Real-time KPIs, pipeline value, conversion funnel, and activity feed — all visible the moment you log in.
Lead Management
Capture, assign, tag, and prioritise every lead. Advanced filters and bulk actions keep your pipeline clean.
Follow-up Scheduling
Log every call, email, and meeting. Set next follow-up dates and never let a lead go cold again.
Export & Import
Download your pipeline as CSV or PDF. Import leads from spreadsheets in seconds with smart column mapping.
Email Notifications
Automated emails on lead assignment, follow-up reminders, and status changes. Fully customisable templates.
Role-Based Access
Admin, Manager, and Staff roles with full permission control. Audit logs track every action for compliance.
Analytics & Reports
Monthly trend charts, conversion funnels, top assignee leaderboards, and priority breakdowns.
Notes & Attachments
Add unlimited internal notes and file attachments to any lead. Full conversation history in one place.
Smart Search & Filters
Full-text search, 8 filter dimensions, saved filter presets, and instant results across your entire pipeline.
